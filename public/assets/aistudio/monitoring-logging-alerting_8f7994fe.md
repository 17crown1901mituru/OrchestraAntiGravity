# Monitoring, logging, and alerting guidelines &nbsp;|&nbsp; Security &nbsp;|&nbsp; Google Cloud Documentation
URL: https://docs.cloud.google.com/docs/security/gcmvsp/monitoring-logging-alerting

The following guidelines for the minimum viable security platform align with the monitoring, logging, and alerting pillar.

Basic level guidelines
----------------------

Implement the following monitoring, logging, and alerting guidelines first.

<table><tbody><tr><th>Item</th><td><h3 id="subscribe-to-security-bulletins" data-text="Subscribe to security bulletins" tabindex="-1">Subscribe to security bulletins</h3></td></tr><tr><th>Description</th><td><div><p>Subscribe to the security bulletin notifications for Google Cloud services to receive alerts about vulnerabilities and mitigation measures.</p><p>When security bulletins are available that are relevant to your Google Cloud service (such as GKE), the service can publish notifications about those events as messages to Pub/Sub topics that you configure. You can receive these notifications on a Pub/Sub subscription, integrate with third-party services, and filter for the notification types you want to receive.</p></div></td></tr><tr><th>Related information</th><td><ul><li><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/support/bulletins">Security Bulletins</a></li></ul></td></tr><tr><th>Item ID</th><td>MVSP-CO-1.54</td></tr><tr><th>Mapping</th><td><p>Related NIST-800-53 controls:</p><ul><li>SI-5</li></ul><p>Related CRI profile controls:</p><ul><li>PR.IP-1.4</li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>

<table><tbody><tr><th>Item</th><td><h3 id="configure-essential-contacts-groups" data-text="Configure Essential Contacts groups" tabindex="-1">Configure Essential Contacts groups</h3></td></tr><tr><th>Description</th><td><div><p>Configure Essential Contacts to ensure that a monitored group alias or mailing list receive important notifications.</p><p>Google sends critical security alerts (like a potential account compromise) to the email addresses listed as Essential Contacts. <table><tbody><tr><th>Item</th><td><h3 id="monitor-billing-anomalies" data-text="Monitor billing anomalies" tabindex="-1">Monitor billing anomalies</h3></td></tr><tr><th>Description</th><td><div><p>Use the billing anomaly feature in Cloud Billing to track any spikes or deviations in expected spend.</p><p>A sudden, unexpected spike in a cloud bill is a primary indicator of a security compromise. Advanced level guidelines
-------------------------

After you implemented the intermediate guidelines, implement the following monitoring, logging, and alerting guidelines.

<table><tbody><tr><th>Item</th><td><h3 id="enable-access-transparency-logs" data-text="Enable Access Transparency logs" tabindex="-1">Enable Access Transparency logs</h3></td></tr><tr><th>Description</th><td><div><p>Standard logs show you what your organization's own users are doing, but Access Transparency logs show what Google support staff do when they access the account. This access typically only happens in response to a support request. Access Transparency logs provide a complete and verifiable audit trail of all access, which is essential for meeting strict compliance and data governance requirements.</p><p>You can enable Access Transparency at the organization level.</p></div></td></tr><tr><th>Related information</th><td><ul><li><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/assured-workloads/access-transparency/docs/enable">Enabling Access Transparency</a></li></ul></td></tr><tr><th>Item ID</th><td>MSVP-CO-1-57</td></tr><tr><th>Mapping</th><td><p>Related NIST-800-53 controls:</p><ul><li>AU-2</li><li>AU-3</li><li>AU-8</li><li>AU-9</li></ul><p>Related CRI profile controls:</p><ul><li>DM.ED-7.1</li><li>DM.ED-7.2</li><li>DM.ED-7.3</li><li>DM.ED-7.4</li><li>PR.IP-1.4</li></ul><p>Compliance Manager control:</p><ul><li><a href="https://docs.cloud.google.com/security-command-center/docs/compliance-manager-cloud-controls#enable-access-transparency">Enable Access Transparency</a></li></ul></td></tr></tbody></table>

<table><tbody><tr><th>Item</th><td><h3 id="export-logs-to-a-log-sink-for-long-term-storage" data-text="Export logs to a log sink for long-term storage" tabindex="-1">Export logs to a log sink for long-term storage</h3></td></tr><tr><th>Description</th><td><div><p>Create a log sink to export logs for your security monitoring solution and set the retention period to meet your requirements.</p><p>The default log retention periods are often not long enough to meet the 1-7 year requirements mandated by compliance regulations like PCI or HIPAA.</p><p>Creating a log sink to export logs to a long-term storage location is essential for meeting certain legal and regulatory obligations.
